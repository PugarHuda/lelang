Bid on a tender

For a human supplier with a browser wallet on Kite testnet. Your bid is sealed: only a hash of it (plus a random salt) goes on-chain with your bond. The page talks to the public API at lelang-6k05.onrender.com; the free instance can take ~50 s to wake up.
